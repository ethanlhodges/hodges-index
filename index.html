Methodology

HodgesIndex measures how many points per 100 possessions a Division I team would outscore an average opponent on a neutral court (Adjusted Efficiency Margin, or AdjEM). Every morning, the model ingests all completed D-I box scores and solves a simultaneous Weighted Ridge Regression across all 365 teams.

  • Tempo-Free & Possession-Weighted: Games are evaluated per 100 possessions (AdjO and AdjD) and weighted by possessions. A separate Ridge solve computes opponent-adjusted pace (AdjT).
  • 3-Point Luck Filtering: Using Empirical Bayes shrinkage toward the 34.0% D-I mean, 15% of single-game 3-point shooting variance is regressed back to each team's baseline before solving the ratings.
  • Garbage-Time Cap & Recency: Single-game margins beyond 35 points per 100 possessions receive diminishing returns (25% slope), paired with a daily recency weight (0.9975/day).
  • Fading Preseason Priors: Early-season ratings blend a 0.90x prior from last season that decays linearly to zero by Game 14 in January. You can uncheck Include prior-season weighting at any time to view pure in-season ratings.
  • Altitude Home Court: In addition to standard home-court advantage (+4.0 pts/100 possessions), high-elevation venues receive a learned +1.55 pts/100 adjustment.
