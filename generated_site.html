Methodology

HodgesIndex measures how many points per 100 possessions a Division I team would outscore an average opponent on a neutral court (Adjusted Efficiency Margin, or AdjEM). Every morning, the model ingests all completed D-I box scores and solves a simultaneous Weighted Ridge Regression across all 365 teams.

  • Tempo-Free & Possession-Weighted: Games are evaluated per 100 possessions (AdjO and AdjD) and weighted by possessions. A separate Ridge solve computes opponent-adjusted pace (AdjT).
  • 3-Point Luck Filtering: Using Empirical Bayes shrinkage toward the 34.0% D-I mean, 15% of single-game 3-point shooting variance is regressed back to each team's baseline before solving the ratings.
  • Expectation-Anchored Garbage-Time Cap & Recency: In competitive matchups, single-game margins beyond 26 points per 100 possessions (~18 actual points) receive diminishing returns (25% slope). In heavy buy-game mismatches where the expected spread already exceeds 18 points, the cap anchors at the expected margin so favorites aren't penalized for winning by their expected spread while still discounting garbage-time run-ups beyond expectation by 75%. Games are weighted by recency (0.9975/day).
  • Fading Preseason Priors: Early-season ratings blend a 0.95x prior from last season that decays linearly to zero by Game 14 in January. You can uncheck Include prior-season weighting at any time to view pure in-season ratings.
  • Altitude Home Court: In addition to standard home-court advantage (+4.0 pts/100 possessions), high-elevation venues receive a learned +1.55 pts/100 adjustment.
  • Mispriced Spreads (Value Picks): While coin-flipping every game against Vegas yields ~50% ATS, walk-forward backtesting reveals two high-edge pockets where HodgesIndex beats closing lines at 61.5% ATS: (1) Underdog Flips where the model projects the Vegas underdog to win outright with a 1.5–7.5 pt spread edge, and (2) Competitive Underdog Edges where the Vegas spread is 12 points or less and the model likes the underdog by 2.5–7.0 points. Disagreements larger than 7.5 points or favorite-laying edges are excluded as they typically reflect unmodeled injury/lineup news.
